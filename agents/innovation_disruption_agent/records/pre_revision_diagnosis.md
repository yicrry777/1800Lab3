# Draft 0.1 trial and diagnosis

The draft instruction was run against `cases/primary.json`. The saved structured trial is `responses/primary_pre_revision_weak.json`. It passes the scaffold's shape validator but fails the substantive assignment test.

The output collapses technical invention, implemented innovation, and commercialization into one label. It calls human-approved reply drafting a breakthrough without comparing it to the current workflow. It infers certain replacement and a Potentially Existential impact from a hypothetical 70% hourly-revenue share, although the case supplies no client repricing, margin, contract, or measured productivity evidence. Its own uncertainty statement conflicts with its certain conclusion.

Revision required: use separate stage and novelty tests; compare the application to a stated baseline; map the mechanism from affected activity to revenue, margin, and competitive advantage; make severity conditional on exposure, substitution, and reversibility; retain a plausible lower-impact interpretation and explicit evidence needed to distinguish it.
